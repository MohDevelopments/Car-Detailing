    Our process, in order

    1. Foam and rinse

      A thick foam lifts loose grit so the paint is never dragged across.

    2. Decontaminate

      Iron remover and a clay bar pull out what washing can’t.

    3. Correct

      Machine polishing removes swirls and light scratches under bright lamps.

    4. Protect

      Sealant or ceramic locks in the gloss and repels water.

    5. Final inspection

      We check every panel in daylight before the keys come back.

    Quick answers

    How long does ceramic coating last?

    With basic care, several years. Your detailer will share a simple maintenance routine.

    Will polishing damage my paint?

    No. We measure paint thickness first and use the lightest method that works.

    Can I stay while you work?

    Of course. Watch from the lounge or drop the car off and relax.
